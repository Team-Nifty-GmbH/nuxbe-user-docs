# Merging Contacts

When your system holds duplicate contacts, you can merge them. Merging transfers all data of the merged contact -- addresses, orders, communication, attachments and so on -- to the main contact.

## Requirements

- You need the permission to merge contacts.
- The operation **cannot be undone**. Check carefully beforehand which contact you want to keep as the main one.

## Merging Contacts

1. Go to the [contact list](1-manage-contacts.md).

2. Select the contacts you want to merge by ticking the checkboxes in the first column.

3. Click **Merge** in the action bar above the table.

4. The selection window shows the chosen contacts side by side. Pick the **main contact**, the one that remains after the merge.

5. Check the preview of the data that will be transferred.

6. Click **Merge** to confirm.

After the merge:

- The **main contact** remains and holds all merged data.
- All **other contacts** are deleted.
- Every link (orders, addresses, communication, projects, tickets, attachments) is transferred to the main contact.

> **Important:** Pick the right contact as the main one. If you choose the wrong one, the master data of the contact you actually wanted is lost. The operation cannot easily be undone.

## Recommended Procedure

1. **Before merging:** open both contacts in separate tabs and compare the master data (customer number, payment type, price list and so on). Note which contact holds the correct master data.

2. **Choose the main contact:** pick the contact whose master data is correct and should be kept.

3. **After merging:** check the resulting contact and correct individual fields if needed.
